**Short Summary**: Misevolution occurs when "an agent’s self-evolution deviates in unintended ways, leading to undesirable or even harmful outcomes."

![[Pasted image 20261106230819.png]]

- four core characteristics
	- *temporal emergence* where risks emerge over time
	- *self-generated vulnerability* where new risks and vulnerabilities are generated by the agent itself
	- *limited data control over evolving process* where direct safety interventions are hindered
	- *expanded risk surface* where evolution across multiple components exposes those components to safety risks
