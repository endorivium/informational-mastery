[Test-Time Compute](https://resources.rework.com/de/libraries/ai-terms/what-is-test-time-compute) (also Inference-Time Compute) is the additional computational power that a model uses to think through a given problem and reach an answer w<span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">i</span>thout the sole use of its parametric training that it has gone through beforehand. Adds multiple intermediate steps to its Reasoning process that leads to the conception and evaluation of multiple solution to find the best one.

[Amortized Analysis](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/dsa/introduction-to-amortized-analysis/) “studies the average cost of operation over a sequence rather than focusing on the worst-case of a single operation” which is too pessimitic as to be a realistic representation of an algorithm’s complexity, e.g. in a dynamic array insertions is O(1) whereas resizing is O(n)  whenever the array runs out of space. There are three techniques; aggregate method which calculates the average cost for an operation, accounting method which assigns credits to ops where cheap ops save credits for future expensive ones and lastly, potential method which defines a potential function representing the stored work in a data structure. Amortized cost in this case is actual cost + change in potential.  
Making something amortizable essentially means that there are many cheap operations that offset one heavy infrequent operation, resulting in a lower average cost per operation.

[Context Saturation](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/context-saturation) “denotes critical regimes in which a structure, representation, or system reaches a maximal or threshold state—either minimizing a form of redundancy or maximizing the use of capacity—such that any further local perturbation induces a global qualitative change”.
