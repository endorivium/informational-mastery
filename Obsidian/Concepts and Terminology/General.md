[Distributed Computing](https://www.readysetcloud.io/blog/allen.helton/distributed-computing-explained-simply-with-ducks/) is where multiple computers distribute a heavy workload amongst themselves to be more fast and efficient. Common issues include exactly-once-delivery (two servers accidentally pick up the same message at the same time and process it twice) and guaranteed order of messages (due to specs, environmental or network related issues, two machines will never take exactly the same amount of time to execute a task).

[Race Conditions](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/operating-systems/race-condition-in-operating-systems/) occurs when two or more processes or threads access and modify the same data at the same time, and the final output depends on the order in which they were run. Causes are: simultaneous access, non-atomic updates which can be interrupted causing inconsistent states, lack of synchronization, improper scheduling.
