Kunst, Comics und Flipcharts

"Making better flipcharts" - what comics can teach us about presenting content in a readable and engaging way

- leading the eye
generally, people read left to right and top to bottom
important not only to consider for single page but multiple pages
 > turning the page creates tension which is relieved when the page is turned
 > first element on page is eye catcher unless it is structured otherwise
   > size of elements pull the eye

- structuring content
for comics, layout and art supports and emphasizes the emotion of the story
 > spacing and layout -> readability, emphasis of certain content
 > panel gutters separate ideas and give breathing space
   > panels convey passage of time and space
 > more horizontal space = more time
 > from art theory: triangles lead the eye 

- simplicity and clear action
icons to underline content, simple shading to create plasticity
 > clear silhouette
 > shading
 > color

but how? do it like the comic artists
> thumbnailing
> medium to thicker pen
