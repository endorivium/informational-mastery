Design Thinking
> iterative interdisciplinary structure that aids in the design of a product/solution

- divergence + convergence: first look at the whole, then narrow it down
- emergence
- design stages: research, ideate, prototype, testing (not necessarily linear, can be repeated or occur simultaneously)
> research (why): understand context and relation to it; gain insight, see advg/disadvg by evaluating existing designs
> ideation (what): consider perspective of all stakeholders (human, economic, technology, environmental), each perspective has a filter that needs to be removed to find the true base which makes innovation possible
> prototyping: experimental stage, fail early and repeatedly to avoid doing so later
> testing: MVP (minimum viable product), key performance metric to evaluate effectiveness and sustainability
