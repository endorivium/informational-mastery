What could be an influencing factor for innovative product architectures?
- modular system architectures have advantages in simplicity and reusability
- understanding of relation and interaction between components (through representative models) is decisive and prerequisite to innovation, should preferably highlight interfaces and interactions between system elements

What are the three steps to form a DMS?
- decompose the system into elements
- understand and document the interactions between the elements (i.e. their integration)
- analyze potential reintegration of the elements via clustering (integration analysis)

What are the four types of system interactions?
- spatial: associations of physical space and alignment; needs for adjacency or orientation between two elements
- energy: needs for energy transfer/exchange between two elements (e.g. power supply)
- information: needs for data or signal exchange between two elements
- material: needs for material exchange between two elements

What does weighting of off-diagonal marks mean?
- off-diagonal square marks are replaced by numbers (coupling coefficient); can be linear or exponential and describe the importance of the interactions between given elements 

What are the steps of integration analysis?
- done via clustering of off-diagonal elements by reordering rows and columns
- foremost objective is to maximize interactions between elements within clusters (chunks) and minimize interactions between clusters, minimization of cluster size
- may be useful to allow overlap where one element may be in more than one cluster
- keep data busses that interact with all clusters out of the matrix
- highly interactive components should be part of a "controls cluster"

What does clustering algorithms mean in that sense?
- reordering of rows and columns to optimize an objective function in the DSM configuration
- objective is defined based on the needs of the system
- e.g. objective: minimize coupling between clusters while minimizing the size of the largest cluster -> DSM has clusters along diagonal
- clustering algorithm should account for the importance of both preluding negative relationships and ensuring positive ones
