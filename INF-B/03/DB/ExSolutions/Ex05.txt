e) The key {LName, LOffice} is unique in all given instances, do not contain null values and point to all tuples within the relation
f) LOffice is unique and does not contain a null value in all given instances, fulfilling the key constraint
g) Since a relational schema splits a relation into subset relations that are interconnected through keys and foreign keys, relational schemas are superkeys of R since it is a subset of R that points to the whole relation
h) EName, ESemester are unique, do not contain a null value and point to the whole relation, fulfilling the superkey constraint
